Mint.com – finance overviews

2008

My buddy Jason Smithson recently posted something about mint.com and I decided to check it out.  It’s a fantastic site, very easy to use, and provides a very simple overview of the categories of my spending.

So, I know I spend X% on food and Y% on rent…

I’ve been nothing but impressed.

There’s also a “how to save money” section which strikes me as advertising and I’ve dutifully ignored it.
