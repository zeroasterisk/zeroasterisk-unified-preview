Poppy is getting older quick

2008

Ok – yeah, she’s only 15 days old today… sounds like nothing… but she is already changing.  Her head is getting more solid and her face seems to be solidifying (for want of a better term).

Poppy is getting big.  She’s still under 10 lbs, but she’s moving more, making want-to-be-crawling motions when on her belly… and is more prone to recognize people around her.

She’s starting to be unhappy when she realizes people aren’t holding her or at least around her… but that might not be new – it’s just new that people aren’t always holding her.

We’re grasping to every moment as best as possible…
