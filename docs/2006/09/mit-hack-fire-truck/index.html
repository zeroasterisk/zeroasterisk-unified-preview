IHTFP Hack Gallery:Fire Truck on the Great Dome:

A buddy of mine who went to MIT sent me this… He said the students have to dodge building security to get to the top of the dome… and the got the fire truck up there by disassembling it, carrying up the parts, and reassembling it on top. 

Amazing!

A lot more effort than most people I know would put into something…
